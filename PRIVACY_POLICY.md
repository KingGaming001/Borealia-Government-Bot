# Borealia Government Bot – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Borealia Government Bot (the "Bot") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when used on Discord.
By using the Bot, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Who This Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- Discord server owners/admins who add or configure the Bot;
- server members who interact with Bot commands, elections, or motions.

## 2. Information We Collect

The Bot is designed to collect only the data needed to provide its functionality.
Depending on usage, this may include:

- **Discord identifiers:** guild (server) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, user IDs, message IDs;
- **Configuration data:** server setup values such as configured channels and roles;
- **Governance records:** election and motion records, nominees, votes, statuses, and timestamps;
- **Operational metadata:** limited logs/errors needed for troubleshooting and service reliability.

The Bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal data categories.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- provide and operate Bot commands and workflows;
- enforce role-based command permissions;
- record election and motion state transitions and outcomes;
- troubleshoot issues, maintain service integrity, and improve reliability;
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Legal Basis (Where Applicable)

Where privacy laws require a legal basis, processing is based on one or more of:

- legitimate interests in operating and securing the Bot;
- performance of a service requested by server administrators/users;
- compliance with legal obligations.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

- Data is stored in the Bot's configured database (SQLite by default for this project).
- Data is retained as long as reasonably necessary for Bot operation, record integrity, dispute handling, or legal compliance.
- Data may be deleted when no longer needed, when a server removes the Bot, or upon verified request where feasible.

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data.
We may disclose data only:

- to service providers/infrastructure used to host or operate the Bot (if any);
- when required by law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request;
- to protect rights, safety, and security of users, servers, or the Bot.

## 7. International Processing

If the Bot is hosted in a jurisdiction different from your own, information may be processed and stored in that jurisdiction.
By using the Bot, you understand that data may be transferred internationally as needed to operate the service.

## 8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored data.
However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data.
To request these rights, contact the owner/operator using the details below.

Server administrators are generally responsible for managing configuration and governance records within their servers.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed to children below the minimum age required by Discord or applicable law.
If you believe data from a child has been provided unlawfully, contact the owner/operator for review and removal where appropriate.

## 11. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may depend on third-party libraries/services.
Those third parties have their own terms and privacy policies, and this Privacy Policy does not control their practices.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
Updated versions become effective when published with a revised effective date.
Continued use of the Bot after updates means you acknowledge the revised policy.
