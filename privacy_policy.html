# Privacy Policy **Borealian Government Bot** Effective Date: 23 February 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Borealian Government Bot (“the Bot”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when used within Discord servers. By inviting or using the Bot, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. --- ## 1. Who We Are Borealian Government Bot is a Discord bot designed to facilitate structured elections, voting systems, and governmental procedures within Discord communities. The Bot operates on the Discord platform and is subject to the policies of Discord. --- ## 2. Information We Collect The Bot collects only the minimum data required to operate properly. ### A. Automatically Collected Data When used in a server, the Bot may collect: * Discord User IDs * Discord Server (Guild) IDs * Channel IDs * Role IDs * Message IDs (when required for vote tracking) * Timestamps of commands and votes ### B. Election & Governance Data * Vote records (linked to User IDs to prevent duplicate voting) * Motion records * Candidate information (if submitted through commands) * Election results --- ## 3. What We Do NOT Collect The Bot does **not** collect or store: * Passwords * Email addresses * Billing information * Personal addresses * Private Discord messages (unless explicitly triggered by a command requiring DM interaction) * Any data outside of what Discord provides to the Bot via its API --- ## 4. How We Use Data Collected data is used strictly to: * Operate election systems * Prevent duplicate or fraudulent voting * Record governance actions * Provide administrative transparency * Maintain system integrity Data is **never** sold, rented, or shared with third parties. --- ## 5. Data Storage & Security * Data is stored securely using reasonable technical safeguards. * Access to stored data is restricted to the Bot developer and system administrators where necessary. * While reasonable security measures are in place, no system is completely immune from risk. --- ## 6. Data Retention Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the Bot’s functionality. Data may be deleted when: * The Bot is removed from a server * A server administrator requests deletion * The Bot is discontinued --- ## 7. Data Deletion Requests Server administrators may request deletion of their server’s stored data by: * Removing the Bot from their server, and/or * Contacting the developer directly Upon verification, server-specific data will be permanently deleted within a reasonable timeframe. --- ## 8. Children’s Privacy The Bot does not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by Discord in their jurisdiction). Use of the Bot is subject to Discord’s age requirements. --- ## 9. Third-Party Services The Bot operates exclusively through the Discord API and is subject to the infrastructure and policies of Discord. We are not responsible for Discord’s data handling practices. --- ## 10. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the Bot after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. --- ## 11. Contact Information For privacy-related inquiries or data requests, contact the Bot developer through: * The official support server (if applicable) * Direct contact via Discord
